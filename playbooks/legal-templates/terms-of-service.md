# Terms of service: subscription variant (template)

*Companion to Chapter 14, "Law, Trust and Safety", of* 1 Person, Billion Dollar Conglomerate *(2026 edition). Last reviewed: September 2026.*

> **STARTING POINT ONLY: requires review by a qualified lawyer before use.** This is not legal advice. Laws on subscriptions, automatic renewal, cancellation, consumer rights and data differ by country and state and change quickly. It is written for a small company selling software or a service by monthly subscription. Every bracket must be replaced, and every clause checked by a lawyer where you and your customers are.

## How to use it

1. Have an agent fill the brackets from your [facts sheet](../../context-kit/facts-sheet.md). Every price, period and rule must come from there, so the two never disagree.
2. Mark any clause you do not understand. Those are your questions for the lawyer.
3. After review, publish on your website, link it from sign-up and checkout, and date it.

The example values in brackets follow the fictional Ledgerly, a bookkeeping-software company that sells monthly plans by card through a hosted checkout, and a done-for-you monthly close service, Ledgerly Assist. For a business that sells goods, use the [terms of sale](terms-of-sale.md) instead.

---

```markdown
# Terms of service
[Company legal name] ("we", "us"), [registered address], [registration number].
Effective: [YYYY-MM-DD]. Version [N].

## 1. Who these terms apply to
These terms apply to everyone who creates an account or subscribes to
[Ledgerly] ("you"). [If you use it for a business, you accept these terms
for that business.] By creating an account you accept these terms.

## 2. What we provide
[Bookkeeping software: bank-feed import, categorization rules, receipt
capture, invoices, a monthly close checklist and reports.] The plans and
what each includes are listed at [link to pricing page].
[If you sell a service:] [Ledgerly Assist] is a done-for-you [monthly close],
described at [link]. What you send, what you receive and by when are set
out there.

## 3. What we do not provide
- [We do not give tax advice or file taxes or returns. Please ask your
  accountant.]
- [We do not move your money or pay bills on your behalf.]
- [Anything else your facts sheet lists under "What we do not do".]

## 4. Trial
[A free trial of 30 days, no card required.] At the end of the trial,
[your account pauses until you choose a plan / describe what happens].
We never start charging without asking you first.

## 5. Plans, prices and billing
- Prices are listed at [link] in [US dollars], [excluding] [sales taxes],
  which are added at checkout where they apply.
- You pay [monthly, by card, through our payment provider's hosted checkout,
  on the day you subscribed]. We do not store card numbers.
- Your subscription renews each [month] until you cancel.
- We will give you at least [60] days' notice by email before a price change
  applies to your existing subscription.

## 6. Failed payments
If a payment fails, we will email you a link to update your card
[and send one reminder]. [Describe what happens next, only as advised by
your lawyer.] We never delete your data because a payment failed.

## 7. Cancelling and refunds
- You can cancel at any time in [Settings], in one step. Cancelling stops
  the next renewal.
- [First month refunded on request within 14 days.] Any other refund is at
  our discretion, decided by [founder name], not by our AI assistant.
- After you cancel, your data stays available to export for [30] days.

## 8. Your data
- Your data is yours. You can export all of it at any time, as [CSV and PDF].
- [Bank connections are read-only.]
- We handle your data as described in our [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md).
- [We do not use your data to train AI models, and our AI providers are
  contracted not to.]
- If you delete your account, we delete your data after [30] days, except
  records we must keep by law, as the privacy policy says.

## 9. AI in our product and support
[AI suggests categories inside the product; you approve them.] Our support
assistant is an AI, will always tell you so, and you can always reach a
person at [contact]. Only [founder name / a named person] can agree prices,
refunds, exceptions or changes to these terms.

## 10. Your responsibilities
- Keep your sign-in details secure, and tell us at once if you think someone
  else has used your account.
- Check the figures before you rely on them for tax or legal filings.
  [Every figure links to the bank lines behind it.]
- Do not use the service unlawfully or to access other people's data.

## 11. Availability and changes
[What you promise about availability, and how you tell customers about
outages, e.g. a status page at [link].] We may change or improve the
service; if a change removes something you rely on, we will tell you [N]
days ahead.

## 12. Our responsibility to you
[Liability clause: to be written with your lawyer. It should say what we
are responsible for, any limit, and what the law does not allow us to
exclude.]

## 13. Ending these terms
[When either side may end the subscription, and what happens to data and
payments when it ends: to be written with your lawyer.]

## 14. Transfer
[If we sell or reorganize our business, these terms and your account may
transfer to the new owner. Wording to be written with your lawyer.]

## 15. Changes to these terms
We will tell you about changes by [email] at least [N] days before they
apply. The version in force is always at [link], with its date.

## 16. Law and disputes
These terms are governed by the laws of [jurisdiction]. [Dispute resolution
clause: to be written with your lawyer.] If you have a problem, contact
[founder name] at [email] first; we will reply within [one working day].

## 17. Contact
[Company name], [address], [email].
```

## Questions to take to the lawyer
- [ ] What must I tell customers before an automatic renewal, and how easy must cancellation be, where my customers are?
- [ ] Do consumer rules apply to some of my customers (sole traders, individuals), and what changes if they do?
- [ ] Is the liability clause right for software that people rely on for their books?
- [ ] Is the price-change notice long enough where I sell?
- [ ] What must I say about AI in the product and in support, and about data used by AI providers?
- [ ] How do these terms and customer accounts transfer if I sell the company?
