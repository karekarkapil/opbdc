# Privacy policy (template)

*Companion to Chapter 14, "Law, Trust and Safety", and Chapter 19, "Building for Value", of* 1 Person, Billion Dollar Conglomerate *(2026 edition). Last reviewed: September 2026.*

> **STARTING POINT ONLY: requires review by a qualified lawyer before use.** This is not legal advice. Data-protection laws differ by country and change. Many require specific wording, specific rights and specific contact details that this template cannot supply. Every bracket must be replaced, and the whole policy checked by a lawyer where you and your customers are.

## How to use it

1. Before filling it in, list what personal data you collect, why, which agents and AI providers see it, and where it is stored. Your [permission matrix](../../stack/permission-matrix.md) is also your privacy map.
2. Read each AI provider's data terms: whether they train on your data, where it is stored, how long it is kept, and whether they will sign a data processing agreement.
3. Fill in the brackets, then take it to your lawyer with the questions at the end.

---

```markdown
# Privacy policy
[Company legal name] ("we", "us"), [address], [registration number].
Effective: [YYYY-MM-DD]. Version [N].
Contact for privacy questions: [name], [email].

## 1. What this policy covers
How we collect, use, store and share personal information about our
customers, their staff, and visitors to [website].

## 2. What we collect
| Information | Example | Why we collect it |
|---|---|---|
| Contact details | [name, email, phone of the ordering manager] | To take and deliver orders, and to contact you about them |
| Business details | [outlet name, delivery address] | To deliver and invoice |
| Order history | [what you ordered and when] | To show past orders and suggest reorders |
| Payment information | [handled by our payment provider; we do not store card numbers] | To take payment |
| Messages | [chat, email and call notes] | To answer questions and improve our service |
| Website data | [describe any cookies or analytics] | [purpose] |

## 3. How we use AI providers
We use AI tools to help run our business. Some of your information is
processed by AI providers acting for us, for these purposes:
- answering your questions through our AI assistant, which tells you it is
  an AI and can pass you to a person;
- preparing order confirmations and routine invoices from your order, at
  our published prices, which are sent without a person checking each one;
- drafting proposals, which a person reviews before anything is agreed;
- [summarizing customer conversations to improve our products].
Our AI providers [do not / do] use your information to train their models.
[Describe where the data is processed and stored, and for how long.]
We have [data processing agreements] with them. The providers we use are
listed at [link].

## 4. Who we share it with
We share information only with the people and services that help us run
the business: [payment provider, delivery partner, accounting software,
AI providers, our accountant]. We do not sell your information.

## 5. If our business changes hands
If we sell, merge or reorganize all or part of our business, your
information may pass to the new owner so that they can continue to serve
you. They will be bound by this policy, or we will tell you before any
change in how your information is used. [Wording to be confirmed with
your lawyer: this is the "consent that travels" item from Chapter 19.]

## 6. How long we keep it
[For each category, the period, and why. For example: order and invoice
records for [N] years, as required for [tax/accounting]; messages for [N]
months.]

## 7. How we protect it
Access is limited to the people and systems that need it. Our AI agents
can see only the records needed for their task, and customer-facing agents
see only the records of the customer they are helping. [Add other measures.]

## 8. Your rights
[List the rights that apply where you and your customers are, such as
access, correction, deletion and objection, and how to use them. To be
written with your lawyer.]

## 9. Changes to this policy
We will post any change here with a new effective date, and tell
[existing customers] by [email] about significant changes.

## 10. Contact
[Name], [email], [address]. [Details of any regulator the law requires
you to name.]
```

## Questions to take to the lawyer
- [ ] Which data-protection laws apply, and what wording and rights must this policy include?
- [ ] Is the AI providers section accurate and sufficient given each provider's terms?
- [ ] Is the change-of-ownership clause enough for customer data to pass to a buyer?
- [ ] Do I need a cookie notice or consent for my website analytics?
- [ ] What must I do, and by when, if customer data is exposed?
