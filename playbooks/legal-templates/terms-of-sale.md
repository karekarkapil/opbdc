# Terms of sale (template)

*Companion to Chapter 14, "Law, Trust and Safety", of* 1 Person, Billion Dollar Conglomerate *(2026 edition). Last reviewed: September 2026.*

> **STARTING POINT ONLY: requires review by a qualified lawyer before use.** This is not legal advice. Laws differ by country, state and industry and change quickly. It is written for a small company selling goods to business customers. Every bracket must be replaced, and every clause checked by a lawyer where you operate.

## How to use it

1. Have an agent fill the brackets from your [facts sheet](../../context-kit/facts-sheet.md). Every price, time and rule must come from there, so the two never disagree.
2. Mark any clause you do not understand. Those are your questions for the lawyer.
3. After review, publish on your website, link it from every order confirmation, and date it.

The example values in brackets follow the fictional Copper Pot Mixers, which sells cocktail mixers to bars and cafes.

---

```markdown
# Terms of sale
[Company legal name] ("we", "us"), [registered address], [registration number].
Effective: [YYYY-MM-DD]. Version [N].

## 1. Who these terms apply to
These terms apply to every order placed with us by a business customer
("you"). We sell to [independent bars and cafes]. We do not sell to
[consumers / large hotel chains]. By placing an order you accept these terms.

## 2. Products and descriptions
Our products and their details (sizes, ingredients, shelf life) are listed
at [link to facts page]. Images show the actual product. If a product is
discontinued, we will tell you before we accept an order that includes it.

## 3. Orders
- The minimum order is [one case of six bottles; mixed cases allowed].
- An order is accepted when we send an order confirmation.
- You may cancel an order within [30 minutes] of placing it, at no charge.
  After that, [describe what happens].
- We may decline an order, for example if a product is unavailable.
  If we do, we will tell you and you will not be charged.

## 4. Prices
- Prices are listed at [link] and are in [currency], [including/excluding]
  [taxes].
- The price that applies is the one shown when we confirm your order.
- We will give you [N] days' notice of any price change to [existing accounts].

## 5. Payment
- Payment is due [on delivery], by [payment link or bank transfer].
- We do not offer credit terms. An invoice unpaid [48 hours] after
  delivery is overdue.
- We will never ask you to pay into a new bank account by email or message.
  If you receive such a request, call us on [phone number] before paying.
- [Late payment: describe any consequence, only as advised by your lawyer.]

## 6. Delivery
- We deliver within [Bengaluru city limits], [Monday to Thursday].
- Each area has a fixed delivery day. Orders received by [Sunday 8 pm]
  are delivered that week; later orders are delivered the following week.
- Delivery is [free for two cases or more; otherwise [amount]].
- Delivery dates are our best estimate. [Describe what happens if we are late.]
- Risk in the goods passes to you [on delivery]. [Ownership passes on payment.]

## 7. Damaged goods and returns
Our [refund and returns policy](refund-policy.md) forms part of these terms.

## 8. Use and storage
Store products as described on the label and at [link]. We are not
responsible for products stored or used contrary to those instructions.

## 9. Our responsibility to you
[Liability clause: to be written with your lawyer. It should say what we
are responsible for, any limit, and what the law does not allow us to
exclude.]

## 10. Things outside our control
[Force majeure clause: to be written with your lawyer.]

## 11. AI assistants
We use AI assistants to answer questions and prepare orders. They will
always tell you they are AI, and you can always reach a person at
[contact]. Only [founder name / a named person] can agree prices,
discounts or changes to these terms.

## 12. Your information
We handle your information as described in our [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md).

## 13. Changes to these terms
We may update these terms. The version in force when we confirm your order
applies to that order. We will tell [existing accounts] about changes
by [email] at least [N] days before they apply.

## 14. Transfer
[If we sell or reorganize our business, these terms may transfer to the
new owner. Wording to be written with your lawyer.]

## 15. Law and disputes
These terms are governed by the laws of [jurisdiction]. [Dispute
resolution clause: to be written with your lawyer.] If you have a problem,
contact [founder name] at [email] first; we will reply within [N] working days.

## 16. Contact
[Company name], [address], [email], [phone].
```

## Questions to take to the lawyer
- [ ] Is the liability clause right for my products and customers?
- [ ] Does anything here fail to apply to consumers if I ever sell to them?
- [ ] Is the cancellation window and pricing notice consistent with the law where I sell?
- [ ] Do I need anything industry-specific (for example food labeling or storage wording)?
- [ ] How do these terms transfer if I sell the company?
