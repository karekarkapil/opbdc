## What we do
Ledgerly is bookkeeping software for micro businesses and the independent bookkeepers who serve them. It turns a month of bank lines and receipts into books you can trust, with every number traceable back to the bank.

## Who we serve
- **Our customer:** owners of micro businesses (one to ten staff) in the US and UK who keep their own books, and independent bookkeepers with 5 to 60 such clients.
- **The moment they need us:** the end of the month, and every Sunday night when the receipts pile up.
- **Not our customer:** companies with inventory accounting, payroll for more than ten staff, or multiple currencies in one set of books (we do not support these well); accountants who need tax-filing software.

## Why we exist
Small businesses lose evenings to matching receipts and bank lines, and still do not trust the result. "I spend every Sunday night matching receipts to bank lines." (customer file, 2025-12-03) "I don't trust a number I can't trace back to the bank." (bookkeeper, 2026-02-17)

## How we make money
- **What customers pay for:** monthly plans (Starter, Plus, Bookkeeper) and the Ledgerly Assist service. Prices are in the facts sheet.
- **How they pay:** card through our hosted checkout, monthly, cancel any time.
- **What it costs us to serve one customer:** hosting, bank-feed connections, model usage for categorization and support, and payment fees; reported per customer in the monthly close.

## What we do NOT do
- We do not give tax advice or file taxes. We say so, and point customers to an accountant.
- We do not move client money or pay bills on anyone's behalf.
- We do not store card numbers. Payments go through a hosted checkout.
- We do not offer phone support. Email and chat, answered by an AI assistant with a person always reachable.
- We do not build custom features for one customer.
- We do not sell customer data, or use it to train anyone's models.

## How we are different
The software is the easy part to copy. Our hard part is trust: every categorization shows why it was made and which bank line it came from, and a qualified bookkeeper stands behind the Assist service. Our proprietary asset is the rules and golden sets built from thousands of real, reviewed months.

## Who does what
- **The founder:** product decisions, prices, every refund, every change to how customer data is handled, the Assist sign-off for each client, and reviewing every code change before release.
- **Agents:** coding agents build from specs (changes reviewed before merge); support agent answers from the facts sheet and help docs (rung 3); categorization suggestions inside the product (customers approve); Assist preparation (rung 1, founder checks); morning operations summary (rung 4).
- **People we work with:** a contract senior engineer (architecture and security review, a few hours a month), an accountant adviser, a lawyer (terms, privacy policy, data processing agreements).
