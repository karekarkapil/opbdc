# Voice guide: Ledgerly

*Companion to Chapters 3, 11 and 12 of* 1 Person, Billion Dollar Conglomerate *(2026 edition). Last reviewed: September 2026.*

*Illustration: Ledgerly is a fictional company.*

Last checked: 2026-09-03

## In one sentence
We sound like a calm, exact bookkeeper who explains things without jargon and never makes you feel silly for asking.

## Principles
1. **Exact.** Numbers, dates and steps, not reassurance. Example: "Your bank feed last updated on 3 September. Click Reconnect in Settings, Banks."
2. **Calm.** Money makes people anxious. No alarm words, no exclamation marks in anything about money.
3. **Plain words.** Say "money in" and "money out" before "credits" and "debits", and explain an accounting term the first time.
4. **Show the source.** When we state a number, say where it came from.
5. **Honest about limits.** "We can't advise on tax" is a complete, kind sentence.

## Words we use / words we avoid
| Use | Avoid | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "your books" | "your financial data ecosystem" | Plain |
| "match" | "reconcile" (in customer replies, until explained) | Owners don't all know the term |
| "I'm Ledgerly's AI assistant" | pretending to be a person | Honesty, and the law in more places each year |
| "Your accountant can advise on that" | any tax opinion | We give no tax advice |
| "effortless", "magic" | | Bookkeeping takes some effort; we make it less |

## A reply we love
> Hi Sam, the $412.50 on 14 August is categorized as "Software" because it matches your rule for that supplier. If it was equipment, change it in the transaction, and tick "apply to future payments" so it sticks. Your profit and loss updates straight away.

Why it works: names the number and date, explains why, gives the exact fix, tells them what happens next.

## A reply we hate
> Great question!! Our powerful AI automatically categorizes all your transactions so you never have to worry about bookkeeping again. Please see our help center for more details.

Why it fails: no answer, a promise we cannot keep ("never have to worry"), sends the customer away.

## By channel
| Channel | Length | Tone adjustments | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Support chat and email | 2 to 5 sentences | Exact, calm | First message says it is an AI assistant and how to reach a person |
| Assist monthly summary | One page | Formal but plain | Signed by the founder |
| Newsletter (monthly) | 400 to 600 words | Teaching | One real lesson from a customer month (with permission) |
| Release notes | One line per change | Neutral | Say what changed and why it matters |
| Help docs | Step by step | Neutral | Screenshots from the real product |

## Things we never say
- Any tax advice or opinion on what is deductible.
- "Guaranteed accurate" or "error-free".
- That we are a regulated accountant or firm.
- A refund or credit the founder has not approved.

## Our disclosure policy (published on the Trust page)
> We use AI tools to build and run Ledgerly, and AI suggests categories inside the product, which you approve. Everything we publish is reviewed by a person. Ledgerly Assist closes are checked and signed off by a qualified bookkeeper. When you talk to our assistant, it will tell you it is an AI, and you can always reach a person. We do not use your data to train AI models.
