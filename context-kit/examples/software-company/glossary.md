# Glossary: Ledgerly

*Companion to Chapter 3, "Context Is the Company", of* 1 Person, Billion Dollar Conglomerate *(2026 edition). Last reviewed: September 2026.*

*Illustration: Ledgerly is a fictional company.*

Last checked: 2026-08-25
Rule for agents: when a term here appears in a message or document, use this meaning. In customer replies, explain accounting terms the first time.

## Industry terms
| Term | What it means here | Not to be confused with | Example in a sentence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reconcile | Check that the books agree with the bank statement, line by line and in the closing balance | "Agree" in the everyday sense | "The June books reconcile to the bank." |
| Bank feed | The automatic, read-only import of bank transactions | A news feed | "Your bank feed stopped on the 3rd." |
| Categorize | Assign a transaction to an account in the chart of accounts | Tagging | "Fuel is categorized as Motor expenses." |
| Chart of accounts | The list of categories the books use | A chart or graph | "Add 'Software' to your chart of accounts." |
| Close (the month) | Finish the month's books so the numbers can be trusted and not changed | Closing the app | "Assist closes your month by the 10th working day." |
| Accounts receivable | Money customers owe the business | Money the business owes | "Two invoices in receivables are over 30 days." |
| Accounts payable | Money the business owes suppliers | | |
| VAT / sales tax | Tax added to sales: VAT in the UK, sales tax in the US | Income tax | "VAT is shown separately on UK invoices." |
| Profit and loss (P&L) | Income minus expenses for a period | Cash in the bank | "Profitable on the P&L, short of cash." |

## Words we avoid
| Word | Why | Use instead |
|---|---|---|
| Deductible, write-off | Tax advice | "Your accountant can advise on that" |
| Guaranteed, error-free | Not true of any bookkeeping | "Checked", "traceable to the bank" |
| Magic, effortless | Hype | Say what the feature does |
