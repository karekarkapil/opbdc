## Who our customers are
Micro-business owners who keep their own books, and the independent bookkeepers who serve them, in the US and UK. Full definition: [`company-brief.md`](company-brief.md).

## The sentences that changed our mind
- "I spend every Sunday night matching receipts to bank lines." (owner, design studio of three, discovery call, 2025-12-03). What it taught us: receipt matching, not reporting, is the pain; receipt capture became the first feature.
- "I don't trust a number I can't trace back to the bank." (bookkeeper, 22 clients, call, 2026-02-17). What it taught us: every figure needs a visible trail; led to "show the source" on every report line.
- "I don't want another app. I want the month to be done." (owner, two-person cleaning company, call, 2026-04-08). What it taught us: some customers want the outcome, not the tool; led to Ledgerly Assist.

## What they have tried
- Spreadsheets with bank exports (most owners; cost: evenings each month).
- A general accounting package "built for accountants, not for me" (owner, 2026-04-08).
- Paying a local bookkeeper by the hour (owner, cleaning company, 2026-04-08). Cost to them: "about $300 a month, and I still send her the receipts."

## What they pay for today
- Bookkeepers pay for one or two tools per client and absorb the cost in their fees (bookkeeper calls, 2026-01 to 2026-03).
