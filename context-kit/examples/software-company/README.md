# Example context kit: Ledgerly

*Companion to Chapter 3, "Context Is the Company", of* 1 Person, Billion Dollar Conglomerate *(2026 edition). Last reviewed: September 2026.*

*Illustration: Ledgerly is a fictional company. Every name, quote, price and date in this folder is invented to show what a filled-in kit looks like.*

## The company in one paragraph

Ledgerly is a small bookkeeping-software company. Its web app helps micro businesses (one to ten staff) and the independent bookkeepers who serve them keep their books: bank-feed import, categorization rules, receipt capture, invoices with payment links, a monthly close checklist and simple reports. It sells in the US and the UK, priced in US dollars. One founder runs it with a staff of agents, a contract senior engineer for a few hours a month and an accountant who advises. It also offers a service, Ledgerly Assist: a done-for-you monthly close, prepared by agents and checked by the founder, a qualified bookkeeper.
