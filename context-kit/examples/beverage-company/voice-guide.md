# Voice guide: Copper Pot Mixers

*Companion to Chapters 3, 11 and 12 of* 1 Person, Billion Dollar Conglomerate *(2026 edition). Last reviewed: September 2026.*

*Illustration: Copper Pot Mixers is a fictional company.*

Last checked: 2026-09-05

## In one sentence
We sound like a good bartender who knows the trade and has time for you: warm, quick, practical, never salesy.

## Principles
1. **Answer first.** The first sentence answers the question. Example: "Yes, Tuesday. Your area's delivery day is Tuesday, and you're before the cut-off."
2. **Bar language, not marketing language.** Say "pour", "serve", "the pineapple", not "elevate your beverage program".
3. **Short.** Bar managers read us on a phone at 1 am. Three sentences is usually enough.
4. **Honest about limits.** If we can't do it, say so plainly and say what we can do.
5. **Real over polished.** Real bars, real drinks, real photographs. A true small story beats a clever line.

## Words we use / words we avoid
| Use | Avoid | Why |
|---|---|---|
| mixer, syrup | "beverage solution" | Nobody behind a bar talks like that |
| the pineapple | "our flagship SKU" | It's what customers call it |
| delivery day | "estimated delivery window" | Customers told us they need the day |
| "I'm Copper Pot's AI assistant" | pretending to be Meera | Honesty, and the law in more places each year |
| "I don't know, let me check with Meera" | a guess | Guesses become promises |
| what is in the bottle | "ultimate", "best" and any superlative we can't prove | Rejected in June and again in August (lessons log, quick entries) |

## A reply we love
> Hi Arjun, yes, we can do that. Four cases of the pineapple and two of the kokum, arriving Tuesday. The pineapple has a new batch date (good until next June). Anything else before I confirm?

Why it works: answers in the first line, repeats the order so mistakes are caught, adds one useful fact, ends with the next step.

## A reply we hate
> Thank you so much for reaching out to Copper Pot Mixers!! We're thrilled you love our handcrafted, artisanal mixers. Our team will review your request and get back to you at the earliest possible convenience. Cheers!

Why it fails: no answer, three empty phrases, exclamation marks, "at the earliest possible convenience" means nothing, and it makes the manager wait.

## By channel
| Channel | Length | Tone adjustments | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Support replies (WhatsApp, email) | 1 to 4 sentences | Plain, warm | First message always says it is an AI assistant and how to reach Meera |
| Complaints about damage | Short | Sorry first, no excuses | Always handed to Meera; she signs every reply |
| Newsletter (monthly) | 250 to 400 words | Story-led | Always opens with a real customer (lesson L-6) |
| Instagram | 1 to 3 lines | Lighter | Real photos only |
| Proposals | One page | Precise, friendly | Prices from the facts sheet only; Meera sends |

## Things we never say
- A delivery time within the day, or a delivery day not in the facts sheet.
- A discount, a refund or a replacement, unless Meera approved it in writing. The only credit the assistant may offer is the capped goodwill credit in the facts sheet, for our own mistake.
- Any health claim, or anything about the alcohol content of a finished drink.
- A competitor's name.

## Our disclosure policy (published on the website's About page)
> We use AI tools to help us write, design and run this company. Everything we publish is reviewed by a person who stands behind it. Photographs of our products are real. We never use generated images of customers or experts, and every testimonial we share comes from a real customer, with their permission. When you talk to an AI assistant on our website or WhatsApp, it will tell you so, and you can always reach a person.
