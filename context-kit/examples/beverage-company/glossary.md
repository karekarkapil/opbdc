# Glossary: Copper Pot Mixers

*Companion to Chapter 3, "Context Is the Company", of* 1 Person, Billion Dollar Conglomerate *(2026 edition). Last reviewed: September 2026.*

*Illustration: Copper Pot Mixers is a fictional company.*

Last checked: 2026-08-20
Rule for agents: when a term here appears in a message or document, use this meaning.

## Industry terms
| Term | What it means here | Not to be confused with | Example in a sentence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Covers | Guests served in an evening | Bottle caps or lids | "We did 180 covers on Friday." |
| 86'd | Out of stock, removed from the menu for tonight | A number or a quantity | "The pineapple mojito is 86'd, we ran out." |
| Par level | The stock a bar wants on the shelf at the start of a shift | An average | "Our par for the pineapple is four bottles." |
| Back bar | The shelves behind the bartender where bottles are displayed | A second bar | "Put the bottles on the back bar, labels out." |
| Speed rail | The rack at the bartender's knee for the most-used bottles | A delivery route | "If it's not on the speed rail, it doesn't get used." |
| Serve | One drink's worth of mixer, 30 ml for us | A customer service interaction | "25 serves per bottle." |
| Jigger | A measuring cup for pouring exact quantities | | "Use a 30 ml jigger." |
| Mocktail | A cocktail-style drink with no alcohol | | "Two mocktails on the new menu." |

## Our internal terms
| Term | What it means | Where it is defined |
|---|---|---|
| The pineapple | Roasted Pineapple and Ginger, our best seller | Facts sheet |
| Case | Six 750 ml bottles; flavors can be mixed | Facts sheet |
| Outlet | One physical bar or cafe; a customer may have several | Company brief |
| Cut-off | Sunday 8 pm; orders after it go to the following week | Facts sheet |
| Delivery day | The fixed weekday on which an area receives deliveries | Facts sheet, delivery schedule |
| Tasting kit | Five 100 ml bottles plus the staff recipe card, free for new accounts | Facts sheet |
| Recipe card | The one-page guide for bar staff with our three most popular drinks | Specifications |
| Tasting visit | Meera visiting to run a staff tasting, for accounts with three or more outlets | Facts sheet |
| Front desk | The support agent on WhatsApp and the website | Agent instructions |
| Goodwill credit | Up to Rs 300 per customer per month, added to the customer's account for a mistake that was ours and taken off the next invoice. Not the same as credit terms, which we never offer | Facts sheet; decision D-8 |

## Words we avoid
| Word | Why | Use instead |
|---|---|---|
| Healthy, natural energy | Health claims we do not make | Describe the flavor |
| Premium, artisanal | Empty; every brand says it | Say what is in the bottle |
| Credit terms, "pay later" | We do not offer credit terms (a goodwill credit is a different thing; use its full name) | "Payment is due on delivery" |
