### L-9: Cafe proposal quantities too high for a new account
- **Date:** 2026-08-31
- **What happened:** the sales agent's overnight proposal for a four-outlet cafe chain suggested ten cases per outlet for the first month, scaled from our largest bar.
- **Who or what:** sales agent, proposal for a multi-outlet account.
- **Caught by:** Meera's review before sending.
- **Cost:** none, a near miss.
- **Lesson:** first orders for new accounts start small and grow from real usage.
- **What changed:** "Proposal for a multi-outlet account" playbook: "first-month quantities: two cases per outlet unless the customer names a number."
- **Trust ladder:** unchanged (proposals stay at rung 2; a near miss is not an incident).

### L-8: Whitefield told "Tuesday"
- **Date:** 2026-07-02
- **What happened:** the support agent gave a Tuesday delivery day to a cafe in an area not on the schedule, by guessing from a nearby area.
- **Who or what:** support agent, routine questions.
- **Caught by:** weekly sample.
- **Cost:** one apologetic call; delivered Wednesday.
- **Lesson:** "other areas" always need Meera to confirm the day.
- **What changed:** support agent instructions, lessons section; facts sheet schedule row made explicit.
- **Trust ladder:** an incident (it reached a customer), so routine questions dropped from rung 3 to rung 1 (Meera approved every reply) and the track record reset to zero. Weekly sample doubled for two weeks. Back to rung 2 on 2026-07-20 and rung 3 on 2026-08-10, one rung at a time, each after thirty replies in a row approved unchanged.

### L-7: Wet boxes, cracked bottles
- **Date:** 2026-06-10
- **What happened:** two cracked bottles in a case delivered in heavy rain; the box had softened.
- **Who or what:** packing (the part-time packer) and the delivery partner.
- **Caught by:** customer.
- **Cost:** the two bottles replaced, and a bar short of them for its weekend.
- **Lesson:** monsoon deliveries need waterproof outer wrap.
- **What changed:** "Packing and dispatch" playbook, step 6: plastic outer wrap June to September; damage tracker started.
- **Trust ladder:** not an agent job (packing is done by a person, rung 0).

### L-5: Research list included a closed bar
- **Date:** 2026-04-15
- **What happened:** the research agent's list of forty bars that opened this quarter included one that had closed. Found by checking five at random against their websites.
- **Who or what:** research agent, bar research.
- **Caught by:** Meera's spot check.
- **Cost:** none.
- **Lesson:** a business listed online may no longer be open.
- **What changed:** "Bar research" playbook, step 4: "confirm each business is still open (recent reviews, posts or a working phone number); mark any you could not confirm."
- **Trust ladder:** unchanged (rung 1).

### L-4: "Opened this quarter" read as "reviewed this quarter"
- **Date:** 2026-04-14
- **What happened:** the research agent's plan searched for bars reviewed in the quarter, not opened.
- **Who or what:** research agent, bar research.
- **Caught by:** reading the plan before it started.
- **Cost:** none; corrected in one sentence.
- **Lesson:** ask for the plan first on every research job.
- **What changed:** research brief template now requires a plan checkpoint.
- **Trust ladder:** unchanged (rung 1).

### L-2: Supplier email asking to change bank details
- **Date:** 2026-03-05
- **What happened:** an email that looked like it came from our bottle supplier asked us to pay a new account. The purchasing agent held it for approval, as instructed.
- **Who or what:** purchasing agent, supplier reorder.
- **Caught by:** agent escalation, then a call to the supplier on the number we already had. It was a fraud attempt.
- **Cost:** none, a near miss.
- **Lesson:** any change to payment details is confirmed by calling on a known number.
- **What changed:** "Supplier reorder" playbook, a controls step (bank-detail changes confirmed by phone on a known number); purchasing agent instructions.
- **Trust ladder:** unchanged (supplier reorders stay at rung 2: money out).
