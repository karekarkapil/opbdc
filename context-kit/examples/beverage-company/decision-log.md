Selected entries shown; the gaps in numbering are entries left out of this example.

### D-10: Weekly social posts move from rung 1 to rung 2
- **Date:** 2026-09-08
- **Decision:** the marketing agent prepares posts ready to go; Meera approves each one before it is published, instead of rewriting each.
- **Reason:** the last thirty drafts were approved with only small edits; the voice guide now has a full rejection log.
- **Alternatives considered:** rung 3 (rejected: public statements never go above rung 2).
- **Revisit if:** any post is published with a factual error.
- **Status:** active. **Files updated:** playbooks index.

### D-9: Reorder page counted a success; phone and WhatsApp orders continue
- **Date:** 2026-07-14
- **Decision:** keep the reorder page as the main path; keep taking WhatsApp orders for managers who prefer them.
- **Reason:** 54 percent of repeat orders came through the page in six weeks; median confirmation under a minute. Three older owners said they will never use an app.
- **Revisit if:** WhatsApp orders drop below a handful a week.
- **Status:** active.

### D-7: No credit terms, including for the cafe chain
- **Date:** 2026-06-20
- **Decision:** payment stays due on delivery for every customer, whatever their size.
- **Reason:** cash forecast shows two months of runway lost if the three largest accounts paid at 30 days. A small company cannot finance its customers.
- **Alternatives considered:** 15-day terms for accounts with six months of history.
- **Revisit if:** runway is above twelve months for two consecutive quarters.
- **Status:** active. **Files updated:** company brief, facts sheet.

### D-6: Subscription delivery retired
- **Date:** 2026-02-20
- **Decision:** stop offering a fixed weekly subscription.
- **Reason:** two sign-ups in three months; every bar said they order to their own stock level.
- **Status:** active. **Files updated:** specifications index (retired).

### D-5: Cut-off moves to Sunday 8 pm
- **Date:** 2026-03-20
- **Decision:** the weekly cut-off moves from Saturday 6 pm to Sunday 8 pm.
- **Reason:** managers do stock on Sunday night after the weekend; "Saturday is our busiest night, I can't think about orders."
- **Revisit if:** the packer cannot prepare Monday's deliveries in time.
- **Status:** active. **Files updated:** facts sheet.

### D-4: No retail sales in 2026
- **Date:** 2026-01-08
- **Decision:** we sell only to bars and cafes this year.
- **Reason:** retail needs different packaging, marketing and support; the trade business is not yet cash-positive every month.
- **Revisit if:** three months of positive cash from trade sales.
- **Status:** active.

### D-2: Agents never offer discounts; Meera's limit is 10 percent
- **Date:** 2025-10-02
- **Decision:** only the founder grants discounts, never above 10 percent.
- **Reason:** margin per case is thin; a discount war with distributors would end the business.
- **Status:** active. **Files updated:** facts sheet, agent instructions.
