## Who our customers are
Owners and managers of independent bars and cafes in Bengaluru, one to five outlets. They order late at night on their phones, care about speed behind the bar, and have little staff time for training. Full definition: [`company-brief.md`](company-brief.md).

## The sentences that changed our mind
- "By the time you answer, I've forgotten what I asked for." (bar manager, two outlets, WhatsApp message, 2026-03-02). What it taught us: late-night reordering needs an instant answer; led to the reorder-in-one-tap spec.
- "My staff don't know what to do with the bottle." (cafe owner, one outlet, visit, 2026-01-20). What it taught us: tasting kits went unused because nobody explained them; led to the one-page staff recipe card.
- "Send me the same as last time, maybe a bit more of the pineapple." (bar owner, three outlets, call, 2026-02-11). What it taught us: most orders are repeats with one change. This is the job the reorder page does.

## What they have tried
- Making syrups in-house from scratch (bar, three outlets, 2025-11-14). Cost to them: about three hours of a head bartender's time each week, by their estimate.
- Buying imported syrups from a distributor (cafe, 2026-01-20). Cost: "double your price, and they don't have kokum."

## What they pay for today
