# Company brief (template)

*Companion to Chapter 3, "Context Is the Company", of* 1 Person, Billion Dollar Conglomerate *(2026 edition). Last reviewed: September 2026.*

## What this is

One page that tells any agent, on any task, what the company is. It is the file most briefs point to first. It covers what the company does, for whom, why it exists, how it makes money and, just as important, what it does **not** do.

## How to use it

- Keep it to one page. If it grows, move detail into the facts sheet, the voice guide or a playbook, and link to it.
- Write for a smart new hire on their first day: explain why, not just what.
- Put facts that customers could be told (prices, delivery times) in the facts sheet, not here. This file links to them.
- Review it quarterly, and whenever you change who you serve or how you make money.

See a filled-in version: [beverage company](examples/beverage-company/company-brief.md), [software company](examples/software-company/company-brief.md).

## Template

```markdown
# Company brief: [Company name]

Last checked: [YYYY-MM-DD]
Owner: [Founder name]

## What we do
[One or two sentences. What you sell, to whom, and the result they get.]

## Who we serve
- Our customer: [Specific type of customer, size, place, situation.]
- The moment they need us: [When and where they use the product.]
- Not our customer: [Who you deliberately do not serve, and why.]

## Why we exist
[The problem, in one or two sentences, ideally with a customer quote from the customer file.]

## How we make money
- What customers pay for: [Product, plan, service.]
- How they pay: [Payment methods and terms. Detail lives in the facts sheet.]
- What it costs us to serve one customer: [Rough figure or "see finance playbook".]

## What we do NOT do
- [We do not ... because ...]
- [We do not ... because ...]
- [We do not ... because ...]

## How we are different
[Two or three sentences. Where the hard part of this business lies: distribution, domain knowledge, trust, physical operations, data, or a service delivered with agents.]

## Who does what
- The founder: [What only you do: pricing, relationships, approvals.]
- Agents: [The main jobs agents do, with their trust-ladder rung.]
- People we work with: [Accountant, lawyer, contractors, partners.]

## Where to find more
- Facts sheet: [link]
- Voice guide: [link]
- Decision log: [link]
- Playbooks: [link]
```

## Guidance notes

- **"What we do not do" is the section agents need most.** An eager agent fills gaps with plausible offers. "We do not offer credit terms" prevents a support agent from promising them.
- **Name the customer precisely.** "Small businesses" is vague. "Independent bars and cafes with one to five outlets" lets an agent judge a new lead correctly.
- **Say why.** "We do not deliver on Fridays because bar kitchens are closed to deliveries during the weekend rush" lets an agent handle cases the rule never mentioned.
- **No secrets.** Bank details, passwords and keys never go in this file.

## Where this breaks

- **Aspirations written as facts.** Describe the company as it is today; put plans in the decision log.
- **Two briefs.** One per company. If you run a portfolio, each company has its own, plus a separate portfolio context.
- **Stale scope.** When you add or drop a customer type, this file changes the same day.
