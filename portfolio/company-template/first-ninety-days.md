# The first ninety days of company two

*Companion to Chapter 16, "The Second Company", of* 1 Person, Billion Dollar Conglomerate *(2026 edition). Last reviewed: September 2026.*

## What this is

A week-by-week plan for starting a second company from the template, and the week-3 setup checklist. With the template, what took weeks the first time takes days. The customer work does not get faster, and should not.

## How to use it

Put the four phases on your calendar before you start. Put the **first company's weekly review** on the calendar first, and do not move it. The commonest failure of a second company is not the second company failing. It is the first one quietly decaying while the founder is distracted.

## Week 3: setup checklist

**Entity and money** (with your lawyer and accountant; this is not legal or tax advice)
- [ ] Legal entity formed, in the form your advisers recommend; held by the holding company if you have one.
- [ ] Bank account in the company's name, used for nothing else.
- [ ] Payment account in the company's name.
- [ ] Accounting software set up, connected to bank and payments ([finance-stack.md](../../playbooks/finance-stack.md)).
- [ ] Tax registrations as advised.

**Everything in the company's name** (Chapter 19)
- [ ] Domain, email, code host, hosting, AI provider accounts, software subscriptions.
- [ ] Trademark search and, when advised, registration.
- [ ] Two-factor sign-in on every account.

**Context kit**
- [ ] Company brief, facts sheet, customer file, decision log, glossary, voice guide, design system, agent instructions, playbooks index, lessons log: all ten created, every `[ADAPT:` and `[EARN:` marker resolved in files agents read.

**Agents**
- [ ] Each agent has its own identity and keys for this company only.
- [ ] New [permission matrix](../../stack/permission-matrix.md) filled in; front-office agents reach only this company's systems.
- [ ] Back-office agents that also serve company one have a separate, limited set of permissions for this company.
- [ ] Spend caps and alerts set ([cost-worksheet.md](../../stack/cost-worksheet.md)).
- [ ] Every job placed on the [trust ladder](../../playbooks/trust-ladder.md) at rung 0 or 1.
- [ ] Kill switch for this company tested.

**Rhythm and records**
- [ ] Added to the portfolio dashboard and the weekly portfolio review.
- [ ] Added to the compliance calendar with its own dates.
- [ ] Data room folder started ([data-room-checklist.md](data-room-checklist.md)).
- [ ] [Portfolio map](../portfolio-map.md) updated.

## Day 90 review

- [ ] Ten paying customers? If not, what did the conversations say?
- [ ] First company: did its dashboard stay green? Any job demoted? Any review skipped?
- [ ] What did the second company add to the shared machine (new skills, better checks)?
- [ ] Decision for the next quarter: grow, keep steady, fix or close. Written in the decision log.
