# Readiness test

*Companion to Chapter 16, "The Second Company", of* 1 Person, Billion Dollar Conglomerate *(2026 edition). Last reviewed: September 2026.*

## What this is

Seven questions that tell you whether your first company can survive a period of your partial absence. Do not start a second company until it can. Boredom with a working first company is a signal, not yet a decision.

## How to use it

Answer in writing, with evidence, for the company you would be stepping back from. If you answer **no to more than one**, the best investment of your attention is still that company: fix the gaps and ask again next quarter. Apply the same test to each company in turn before adding another (a third company before the second is stable is a failure mode of its own).

## Fixing the common gaps

| Gap | Where to start |
|---|---|
| 1. Cash | [Monthly close](../../playbooks/monthly-close.md) and the twelve-month cash forecast |
| 2. Review over two hours | [Weekly review](../../playbooks/weekly-review.md): is the agent preparing the pre-read? Are escalations batched? |
| 3. Jobs stuck at low rungs | [Trust ladder](../../playbooks/trust-ladder.md): which jobs have a track record but no promotion decision? |
| 4. Stale context | [Context kit](../../context-kit/README.md): the monthly context review |
| 5. No incident tested | [Incident runbook](../../playbooks/incident-runbook.md): run a drill, such as a backup restore |
| 6. Customers cannot reach you | [Support agent](../../briefs/support-agent.md): the handover rules and "always a way out" |
| 7. Not rested | The founder's calendar in Chapter 15: protect off time before anything else |

## Then choose which company

Run every candidate through the [validation scorecard](../../playbooks/validation-scorecard.md). Prefer adjacent second companies:

- **Same customer, new problem:** you start with trust, distribution and insight.
- **Same machine, new customer:** the support playbook, content engine and finance routine carry over; the problem must still pass the scorecard.
- **Something entirely new:** possible, but it reuses the least. Treat it as a first company.

The second company should strengthen the machine, not merely use it.
