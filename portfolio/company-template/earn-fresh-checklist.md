# Earn fresh: the never-copy checklist

*Companion to Chapter 16, "The Second Company", of* 1 Person, Billion Dollar Conglomerate *(2026 edition). Last reviewed: September 2026.*

## What this is

The things that must be earned fresh, every time. None of them may be copied from your first company, and no agent may fill them from company one's files. This is the list that protects you from the most dangerous mistake in a second company: assuming the new customers are like the old ones.

## How to use it

Tick an item only when you can point to the evidence. Keep this file in the new company's folder; it becomes the first entry in its decision log.

---

New company: [name]

## 1. The customer insight

- [ ] A research brief run for the new market, with verbatim quotes and links ([research agent](../../briefs/research-agent.md)).
- [ ] **Ten real conversations** with people who have the problem, each at least twenty minutes, held by you ([customer conversations](../../playbooks/customer-conversations.md)). Dates: [list]
- [ ] A new **customer file**, started from those conversations, with verbatim quotes and dates.
- [ ] The new company's own **sentence on the napkin**, written down: "[quote]", [date], [who].
- [ ] What you assumed from company one that turned out to be wrong: [list]. (If the list is empty, look again.)

## 2. The facts sheet

- [ ] Every price set for this company, not carried over.
- [ ] Every policy written from scratch: delivery, returns, refunds, payment terms, discounts.
- [ ] Every promise checked: what this company will and will not do.
- [ ] Contact details, hours, service areas for this company.
- [ ] "Last checked" date on the file: [YYYY-MM-DD]

## 3. The relationships

- [ ] First customers met personally, by call or in person ([first ten](../../playbooks/first-ten.md)).
- [ ] Suppliers and partners met, and contracts signed in the new company's name.
- [ ] Advisers for the new industry identified (a specialist lawyer if the industry has its own rules).

## 4. The decision to start

- [ ] A new [validation scorecard](../../playbooks/validation-scorecard.md), honestly filled in, passing the gate (3 or more on severity, willingness to pay and reachability).
- [ ] Question 9, machine fit, answered: which parts of the machine this company reuses and which it strengthens.
- [ ] The first company passed the [readiness test](readiness-test.md) this quarter.
- [ ] The decision and its reasons written in the new company's decision log.

## 5. Trust, on the ladder

- [ ] Every recurring job in the new company starts at rung 0 or 1, whatever its rung in company one.
- [ ] Each job's track record counted from zero in the new company.

## Warning signs you copied what should have been earned

- An agent quotes a price, policy or delivery term that belongs to company one.
- The new customer file contains quotes from company one's customers.
- The new company's golden set uses company one's cases.
- You describe the new customers using company one's words.

Each of these goes into the new company's lessons log, with the fix.
