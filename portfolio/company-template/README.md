# Company template

*Companion to Chapter 16, "The Second Company", of* 1 Person, Billion Dollar Conglomerate *(2026 edition). Last reviewed: September 2026.*

## What this is

A guide to packaging your first company's machine as a template, so the second company can start with it on day one: the copy-as-is list, the keep-but-adapt list with the placeholders to mark, the checklist of what must be earned fresh, the readiness test and a ninety-day plan. Chapter 16 sorts everything from company one into three groups:

- **Copy as is:** carries across almost unchanged.
- **Keep, but adapt:** the structure carries across; the content is new. Placeholders mark exactly what to change.
- **Never copy:** must be earned fresh, every time.

The template does not duplicate the rest of this repository. It points to the files you already use, and tells you what to do with each one.

## How to use it

1. **Take the [readiness test](readiness-test.md)** for your first company. If you answer no to more than one question, stop here and fix the gaps.
2. **Choose the second company** with the [validation scorecard](../../playbooks/validation-scorecard.md), paying special attention to question 9, machine fit. Prefer adjacent: the same customer with a new problem, or the same machine with a new customer.
3. **Make a new company folder** (outside this repository, in your own files), for example:

   ```
   [new-company]/
     context-kit/      from ../../context-kit/ templates (adapt or write fresh, per the lists)
     briefs/           from ../../briefs/ (copy; adapt examples)
     playbooks/        from ../../playbooks/ (copy; adapt the marked files)
     stack/            permission matrix and cost worksheet for THIS company
     data-room/        per data-room-checklist.md, from day one
   ```

4. **Work through [copy-as-is.md](copy-as-is.md)**, then **[keep-but-adapt.md](keep-but-adapt.md)**, replacing every placeholder.
5. **Work through [earn-fresh-checklist.md](earn-fresh-checklist.md).** Nothing on it may be copied from company one.
6. **Follow [first-ninety-days.md](first-ninety-days.md).** Keep the first company's weekly review on the calendar, unmoved.

## The placeholder convention

In the adapt list and in your new company's files, anything that must change is marked:

- `[ADAPT: what to change]`: rewrite for the new company before any agent reads the file.
- `[EARN: what to find out]`: may not be filled from company one at all; it comes from the new company's own conversations, research or decisions.

Before an agent works on the new company, search its folder for `[ADAPT:` and `[EARN:`. The count must be zero, or every remaining marker must be in a file no agent reads yet.

## The rule this template protects

> The most dangerous mistake in a second company is to copy what should have been earned: to assume the new customers are like the old ones, or to let agents carry a policy from company one into company two where it does not belong.

And from Chapter 17: **share know-how, separate data and money.** The template carries know-how only. No customer data, credentials, bank details or contracts ever travel through it.
