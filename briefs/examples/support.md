# Example brief: support

*Companion to Chapters 3, 4 and 12, "Context Is the Company", "Managing Agents" and "Sales and Support Agents", of* 1 Person, Billion Dollar Conglomerate *(2026 edition). Last reviewed: September 2026.*

*Copper Pot Mixers is a fictional company used for illustration.* This is a one-off brief, not the standing support agent (that lives in [support-agent.md](../support-agent.md)). After a week of monsoon rain, three bars have reported damaged bottles. The support agent has already sent each of them a holding acknowledgement and collected the photos, as the damaged-shipment playbook says. Replying to a complaint about a damaged shipment sits at rung 1 of the trust ladder: the agent drafts, and Meera, the founder, signs every reply.

## The brief

> **Goal:** Draft a reply to each of this week's three damaged-shipment reports, with a replacement order for each, so I can check, sign and send all three in one sitting before noon today. These bars lost drinks before a weekend; a quick, plain, personal reply keeps them.
>
> **Context:** Read the three conversations and photos in the "For Meera" list, each bar's last three orders, the facts sheet ("Returns, refunds and problems", the delivery schedule and the cut-off), the damaged-shipment playbook, the voice guide ("Complaints about damage": short, sorry first, no excuses), and the damage tracker "damages-2026" for these deliveries. Lessons log L-7 explains the monsoon wrap.
>
> **Constraints:** Draft only; send nothing, and do not contact the bars again. In each draft, promise only what the facts sheet allows: a free replacement of the damaged bottles, on the area's next delivery day from the schedule. No refund, discount or credit of any kind (damage in transit is my decision, never a goodwill credit), no delivery outside the schedule, no blame on the delivery partner or the customer. Replacement orders stay as drafts marked "replacement, no charge", held for my approval.
>
> **Done:** For each bar: a reply of three to five sentences in my voice, ready for me to sign, and a draft replacement order with its delivery day. Then a one-paragraph note: anything unusual (a report later than 48 hours after delivery, a photo that does not show the damage, a bar with an earlier damage report), and whether the three share a delivery date or route.
>
> **Verification:** For each draft, show the facts-sheet line behind every promise, the delivery date and the time of the report (to confirm the 48 hours), and the order line the replacement quantity comes from. Confirm the replacement equals the damaged bottles, not the whole order.
>
> **Questions:** For a report later than 48 hours after delivery, prepare the case but leave the replacement promise out of the draft, and ask me; never refuse. Stop and ask me at once, marked urgent, for any mention of glass in a drink, an injury, a lawyer or a regulator (mark it urgent), or if a bar asks for something the facts sheet does not cover.

## Why this brief works

- **The agent drafts; the founder signs.** The reply carries a promise and a relationship, so it stays at rung 1, exactly as the damaged-shipment row of the trust ladder says.
- **The promise is bounded by the facts sheet.** The draft cannot offer what the company does not do: no credit, no refund, no special delivery.
- **Evidence for every promise** (the 48-hour check, the order line, the schedule) turns a ten-minute review into three quick signatures.
- **The pattern question** (same date, same route) turns three complaints into a lesson for the packing and delivery playbooks.

## What to check when it comes back

- [ ] Read each reply aloud. Does it sound like you, sorry first, with no excuses?
- [ ] Check one delivery day against the schedule yourself, and one replacement quantity against the photo.
- [ ] Anything later than 48 hours: decide it yourself. The facts sheet sets the rule; you may still choose to replace.
- [ ] Approve the replacement orders, then send the replies from your own account.
- [ ] Nothing was sent by the agent. Check the sent folder.
- [ ] Record: if the three share a route or date, add a line to the lessons log and tell the delivery partner.
